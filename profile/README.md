<h2><img src="assets/icons/cat.svg" width="28" height="28" alt="" /> Playing as a cat</h2>

Nekogenix lets you play as a neko or a cat and keep using your items, armour and elytra. The transformation happens gradually. You can stop it if you’d rather keep the neko form.

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/cat.gif" alt="A cat player with armour, elytra, a pickaxe and a torch in a cave" width="640" />
</p>

<h3><img src="assets/icons/paw-print.svg" width="24" height="24" alt="" /> Abilities</h3>

Double-jump, see in the dark, take less fall damage and find creatures by scent. Creepers keep their distance, and you can stay awake without spawning phantoms.

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/abilities.gif" alt="Gameplay showing a double-jump, feline vision, a high fall and scent outlines" width="640" />
</p>

<h3><img src="assets/icons/flask-conical.svg" width="24" height="24" alt="" /> Laboratory</h3>

Prepare your injection with syringes, saliva samples, a centrifuge and a pneumatic stand. The [laboratory guide](https://nekogenix.github.io/wiki/laboratory/) has the recipes and steps.

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/laboratory.gif" alt="Using the centrifuge and pneumatic stand to prepare the injection" width="640" />
</p>

<h3><img src="assets/icons/palette.svg" width="24" height="24" alt="" /> Cat skins</h3>

Pick a vanilla cat coat or import your own skin. Your choice also changes the ears in neko form.

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/skins.gif" alt="Choosing and previewing cat skins in the in-game skin menu" width="640" />
</p>
